## Take 2 — everything after the reveal

Voice: Kore. Style: `confident, warm and upbeat product-launch narrator, smiling, crisp energetic pace`

```
So we built something better. <long pause> This is Epoch Medical System.

One system for the WHOLE clinic. Reception, doctor, pharmacy and administration. One address, one sign-in, and everyone lands in exactly the workspace they need.

Patients are found in seconds, registered once, linked to their families, and placed in one live queue. Walk-in or booked, nobody gets lost.

In the consultation room, the full history is on one screen. Diagnoses are coded with I-C-D eleven, searched as you type, even offline. Lab tests, follow-ups, and a clear, typed prescription, in just a few clicks.

And before the patient even reaches the counter, the prescription is already waiting. Epoch picks the batch that expires first, tracks every tablet, and prints the label.

The bill builds itself. Payments, balances, and the day's cash count, all reconciled.

And it all stays INSIDE the clinic. It runs on your own network, keeps working when the internet doesn't, logs every change, and backs itself up, encrypted.

One visit. <short pause> One record. <short pause> Zero chaos. <long pause> Epoch. Your clinic, connected.
```
