How a visit flows

From the front door to the medicine counter

Every step is linked to the same visit, so nothing is copied by hand and nothing goes missing.

  1. 1

    Reception

    Find or register the patient by name, phone or NIC. Add them to today's queue and print a token.

  2. 2

    Doctor

    Call the next patient. Record vitals, findings and diagnosis. Allergies are shown before you prescribe.

  3. 3

    Pharmacy

    The prescription appears on the dispensary screen. The batch expiring first is picked automatically.

  4. 4

    Billing

    Consultation fee and dispensed medicines are on one invoice. Take cash, card or a split payment.
